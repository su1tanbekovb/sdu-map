How to use the map

Type a place in “To”: library, canteen, D110, F307, relax room. The route starts at the Main Entrance or at any point you choose in “From”.

Building

  • One vertical corridor connects blocks I, H, G, F, E, D and C. Each block has a lift (⇅) in the middle of the block, not on the corridor.
  • Floors 1–3: 10 rooms per block, e.g. D101–D110, F301–F310. Blocks G, H and I add 5 rooms on the east side (G111–G115, G211–G215 …). On floor 1 the rooms G113–G115 are replaced by the Mini Red Hall. On floor 3 the east side of H holds the relax rooms instead.
  • Floor 4: 5 rooms per block, on the top row, e.g. F401–F405.
  • There are 4 barrels (round lecture halls, all the same size) in a zigzag from Block C: one at C, two at D and one at E. On floors 1 and 2 each barrel has one room: C117, D117, D118, E117 (floor 2: C217, D217, D218, E217). On floor 3 the barrels have no rooms: each is just a roof where students can study.
  • The Market (floor 1 only) and the Canteen are together on the east side of Block F, the Market above the Canteen. On floors 2 and 3 there is no Market, so the Canteen is bigger.
  • Level −1 has the same layout as the other floors: every block has a room 01 on its top row, at the far left (D01 … I01). Block H also has H02, H03 and the H Canteen.
  • Student Service and the SDU Store (clothing shop) are below Block D, one under the other.
  • Library is in Block B. The Red Hall is to its left, the round Wi-Fi Zone to its right.
  • Relax rooms (men’s and women’s) are in Block H on floor 3.

Accounts

Sign up or log in with email and password (top right). Members (students) browse the map, join events, add friends (the other person must accept), see each other’s profiles (e-mails stay private) and edit their own profile. Admins can also create and delete events. Admins are chosen only by the site owner, in the database.
